The operating gap

CRA readiness is not only a checklist.

Evidence

Teams need product-version SBOM evidence, content hashes, retained files, and analysis history they can explain later.

Workflow

Article 14 operations require awareness timestamps, 24-hour and 72-hour stages, final-report triggers, and review gates.

Accountability

Scanner output is not enough. Reviewers and approvers need to preserve affectedness decisions, payloads, and audit trails.
